# brand.md

## Colors
- Background cards: #000000 at 85% opacity, 32px corner radius
- Text: #FFFFFF
- Accent: #FF3B30, used on keywords only

## Type
- Font: Inter Bold (`assets/fonts/Inter-Bold.ttf`)
- Headlines: 120px, ALL CAPS, max 2 lines, max width 820px (shrink to fit, never below 80px)
- Captions: 64px, ALL CAPS, 3 words max per line, 6px black stroke, keyword in accent

## Placement (1080x1920)
- Graphics: y 220–900
- Captions: y 1000–1180 (never overlapping graphics or the mouth)
- Safe zones: nothing in top 220px, bottom 400px, right 130px

## Motion
- Entry: scale 0 → 110% → 100% over 6 frames, landing on the trigger word
- Exit: 4-frame fade, or cut when the next graphic lands
- No transitions longer than 4 frames

## Audio
- Music: `assets/music/`, 12 dB under the voice, 1s fade-out
- Master: -14 LUFS integrated, -1 dB true peak
